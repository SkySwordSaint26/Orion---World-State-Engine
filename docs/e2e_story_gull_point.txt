Chapter 1: The Keeper

Mara Quinn was forty-one years old and had kept the lighthouse at Gull Point for eleven winters. She had green eyes, a quiet voice, and hands that smelled of lamp oil. Mara was born in Port Averly, a fishing town two hours down the coast, but she had not been back there since her mother's funeral.

Her daughter, Lily Quinn, was nine years old. Lily spent her afternoons on the rocks below the tower, counting the ships that passed. Mara's brother, Tobias Quinn, was a fisherman. He lived in a small cottage near the harbor and rowed out every morning before dawn.

Captain Elias Brandt was Mara's enemy. Years ago he had run his ship onto the reef because he ignored her signal, and he had blamed her in front of the whole Harbor Council. Doctor Hanna Weiss worked for the Harbor Council. She was the only person in town who still spoke to both of them.

On the first night of the season, Hanna arrived at the lighthouse with a letter from the Council. The letter said the lamp would be replaced with an automatic light by spring.

Chapter 2: The Storm

A week later a storm came in from the north. Tobias was still at sea when the wind turned. Mara climbed the tower and kept the lamp burning all night, while Lily watched the dark water from the window.

At dawn Tobias rowed into the harbor, soaked but alive. Captain Brandt was not so lucky. His ship broke apart on the reef, and the men from the harbor found his body on the beach that afternoon. Elias Brandt was dead.

Hanna Weiss examined the body and wrote the report for the Harbor Council. After the funeral, Tobias told Mara that Brandt had been trying to reach the lighthouse when the ship went down.

Chapter 3: The Letter

In the spring Mara travelled to Port Averly to speak to the Harbor Council. She was thirty-eight years old, she told the clerk, and she had kept the light longer than anyone alive. The clerk looked at her grey eyes and wrote nothing down.

Lily stayed with Tobias while her mother was away. Lily was ten years old now, and she helped her uncle mend his nets every evening. Hanna Weiss visited them twice and brought bread from the bakery.

When Mara returned to Gull Point, she found a second letter from the Council waiting on the table. The automatic light would arrive in June.

Chapter 4: The Visitor

In June a stranger walked into the tavern at Gull Point. It was Captain Elias Brandt, alive and well, with a new coat and an old scar across his cheek. The whole room went silent.

Brandt was Mara's oldest friend, he told the room, and he had come back to help her keep the lighthouse. Mara said nothing. She had been born at Gull Point, she said later to Hanna, and she would die there too.

That night Mara, Tobias and Lily climbed the tower together and lit the lamp for the last time.
